MEDICATION INFORMATION
Personal safety support

This page gives safe next steps, not a diagnosis, prescription or medication recommendation.

WHAT TO DO NEXT

Get a personal medication review from a professional.

AuditDDI does not ask for your health information or tell you whether to take a medicine combination.

Prepare

Bring all medicines, strengths, supplements, allergies and recent changes to your pharmacist or prescriber.

Ask

Ask them to check the full list against your conditions, dose, timing and test results.

Get urgent help

For suspected overdose, severe symptoms or an emergency, contact local emergency services immediately.
