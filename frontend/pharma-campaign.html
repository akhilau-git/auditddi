CAMPAIGN PLANNING

Prepare a governed screening campaign.

Establish compound provenance, the assay question, review owner, and export requirements before any high-throughput compute is scheduled.

1 · Controlled input

Use canonical SMILES, immutable compound identifiers, source provenance and versioned assay intent.

2 · Governed execution

Batch execution requires a queue, durable results storage, quotas and access-controlled exports.

3 · Experimental review

Use this system to prioritise assays, never to assert safety, efficacy, investability or approval.

Deployment status: single-pair evidence is operational. Bulk campaign execution remains deliberately unavailable until the deployment has approved ingestion, queueing and retention controls.
